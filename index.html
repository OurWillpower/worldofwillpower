About the group

Built on Clarity, Not Hype

From one founder to many brands

World of Willpower started in 2012 when Jayant Vishnu Bhat began mentoring founders who wanted more than motivational speeches. The work naturally expanded into setting up plants, brands and go-to-market systems.

Today, the group spans mentoring, dairy, food, Ayurveda, technology, investor partnerships and aggregators — but the spine is the same: clean thinking, practical design and disciplined execution.

Every vertical is run with a “boardroom + shop-floor” mindset — understanding strategy at the top and reality at the ground.
