About World of Willpower

A Group Built Around Execution, Quality and Long-Term Thinking.

In 2012, Mr. Jayant Bhat started Willpower with a simple but demanding idea: serious founders and customers deserve serious thinking. Over the years, this expanded into a group of ventures in dairy, foods, Ayurveda, software, training, investments, logistics and financial planning.

Each company inside the World of Willpower is designed with one principle: if you want the best possible version of that product or service – from pure cow ghee and herbal formulations to technology systems and curated investment structures – you should be comfortable choosing Willpower.
