  • Flagship products like Betatic, Herbal Punch, Drona Ghee, Snova goat-milk soap and targeted herbal capsules.

Product Families

The exact SKUs and packaging can evolve, but the direction stays the same – calm, serious products designed for repeated use by the same family, not just for one-time trials.

DIABETES & METABOLIC SUPPORT
Betatic & Related Tonics

Herbal formulations focused on blood sugar management, metabolic support and long-term discipline – aligned with a serious approach to diabetes care.

Intended for long-term use under proper guidance – not “magic cure” positioning.

HERBAL JUICES
Herbal Punch & Aloe Combinations

Aloe vera with combinations like karela, jamun, neem, moringa, amla and others – for gut health, daily vitality and overall wellness.

Each combination has a clear internal logic – not random herb lists.

DAIRY & TRADITIONAL FATS
Drona Pure Desi Cow Ghee

Ghee built on purity, traceability and respect for traditional fats – extending Willpower Dairy Care into homes that care about both taste and long-term health.

Planned SKU structure that can work across retail, e-commerce and institutional buyers.

SKIN & BATH CARE
Snova Goat Milk Soap Range

Goat-milk based soaps with supporting oils like coconut and castor – built for a soft, comfortable skin feel, especially for people who are careful about what touches their skin.

Can be extended into variants for different skin preferences over time.

HERBAL CAPSULES
Targeted Nutraceutical Capsules

Pure, non-gimmicky capsules built for specific health needs – metabolic, kidney, general wellness and more – prioritising ingredient quality and documentation.

Designed for people who read labels and ask detailed questions.

COMING ADDITIONS
Future Health & Lifestyle Lines

As the Willpower ecosystem grows, new product concepts will come here only when they are ready to stand scrutiny – not just because a category is trending.

Better to launch slowly with clarity than quickly with confusion.
