About

Who We Are.

World of Willpower started in 2012 as a focused mentoring and consulting initiative for founders. Over time, the vision expanded into tangible businesses across dairy, foods, Ayurveda-based wellness, software and digital solutions, investors’ platforms and aggregators.

The common thread across all ventures is disciplined execution, transparent partnerships and a relentless approach to quality — whether it is a bottle of ghee, an Ayurvedic formulation, a web application or a mentoring engagement.

Philosophy

The Willpower Way.

Every venture under World of Willpower is built with:

  • Clear focus on real value for customers and partners
  • Premium positioning with honest, execution–backed promises
  • Ability to think like both an entrepreneur and an operator
  • Long–term relationship mindset over transactional deals
