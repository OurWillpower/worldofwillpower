WORLD OF WILLPOWER · ABOUT THE GROUP

Long-Term Thinking Over Quick Noise.

World of Willpower was born from a simple observation: many people want to build brands, but very few are willing to do the real, disciplined work behind them. Over the years, the group has grown into multiple ventures – mentoring, dairy, foods, Ayurveda, technology, capital and money – all driven by one mindset: long-term thinking over quick noise.

Real, Disciplined Work Behind Every Brand. One Philosophy Across Many Verticals. Calm, Serious and Documentation-Driven.

HOW THE GROUP OPERATES
  • Each venture is different, but the core remains: disciplined thinking, documentation and respect for the end customer.
  • From mentoring to manufacturing, everything is anchored to practical execution, not just presentations.
  • We prefer steady, compounding progress instead of chasing every market trend or slogan of the month.
  • Partners, investors and customers are treated as long-term relationships – not as one-time transactions.

How World of Willpower Evolved

From a practical need to a full group of companies.

The journey of World of Willpower started with a practical need – founders and professionals required someone who could think through strategy, documentation and execution without adding noise. What began as mentoring and consulting gradually layered into dairy, foods, Ayurveda, technology, capital and money advisory – wherever the Willpower mindset could add value.

Across each vertical, the approach has remained consistent:

  • Clarity in positioning and purpose.
  • Respect for regulations, documentation and quality benchmarks.
  • Products and services that can stand scrutiny, not just marketing lines.
  • Partners who understand that brand-building is a long-term game.

About the Founder

The thinking style behind the Willpower philosophy.

In the year 2012, World of Willpower began to take shape under the experience and thinking style of Dr. Jayant Vishnu Bhat, MD (Naturopath), M.Sc. Cytogenetics – a founder who has seen multiple sides of business: corporate roles, manufacturing, brand-building, consulting and now a group of ventures under one calm, serious identity.

Jayant has had the opportunity to work with large conglomerates like Troikaa Pharma, Raptakos Brett, ICICI Prudential Life, MetLife (Reinsurance Group of the Americas), Reliance and ING Life at decisive levels. This exposure to different industries and decision stages shapes how World of Willpower thinks and works.

The same mindset flows into every Willpower venture – whether it is dairy purity, Ayurvedic formulations, technology systems or financial planning. The focus is always on long-term trust, real value on the ground and work that can be defended, documented and improved over time.
