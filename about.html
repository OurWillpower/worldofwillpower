About World of Willpower

World of Willpower was born from a simple observation: many people want to build brands, but very few are willing to do the real, disciplined work behind them. Over the years, the group has grown into multiple ventures – mentoring, dairy, foods, Ayurveda, technology, capital and money – all driven by one mindset: long-term thinking over quick noise.

The journey of World of Willpower started with a practical need – founders and professionals required someone who could think through strategy, documentation and execution without adding noise. What began as mentoring and consulting gradually became dairy, foods, Ayurveda, technology and money advisory – wherever the Willpower mindset could add value.

Our Approach

  • Clarity in positioning and purpose
  • Respect for regulations, documentation and quality benchmarks
  • Products and services that can stand scrutiny, not just marketing lines
  • Partners who understand brand-building is a long-term game

Leadership

Founded in 2012, World of Willpower has been shaped by the experience and thinking style of Dr. Jayant Vishnu Bhat, MD (Naturopath), M.Sc Cytogenetics – a founder who has seen multiple sides of business: corporate roles, manufacturing, brand-building, consulting and now a group of ventures under one calm, serious identity.

Jayant has had the opportunity to work with large conglomerates like Troikaa Pharma, Raptakos Brett, ICICI PruLife, MetLife India Reinsurance Group of Americas, Reliance and ING Life at decisive levels. This mindset flows into every Willpower venture, whether it is dairy purity, Ayurvedic formulations, technology systems or financial planning.
