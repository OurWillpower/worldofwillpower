ABOUT · WORLD OF WILLPOWER

About World of Willpower – Calm, Serious Brand-Building.

World of Willpower was born from a simple observation: many people want to build brands, but very few are willing to do the real, disciplined work behind them.

Over the years, the group has grown into ventures across mentoring, dairy, foods, Ayurveda, technology, capital and money – all driven by one mindset: long-term thinking over quick noise.

HOW WE TYPICALLY THINK
  • Strategy, documentation and execution – without extra motivational noise.
  • Products and services that can stand scrutiny from regulators, consumers and partners.
  • Respect for regulations, audits and quality benchmarks in every category we touch.
  • Partners who understand that brand-building is a long-term game, not a monthly campaign.

How the Willpower Journey Evolved

The journey of World of Willpower started with a practical need – founders and professionals required someone who could think through strategy, documentation and execution without adding noise.

What began as mentoring and consulting gradually layered into dairy, foods, Ayurveda, technology, capital and money advisory – wherever the Willpower mindset could add value. Across each vertical, the approach has remained consistent:

  • Clarity in positioning and purpose
  • Respect for regulations, documentation and quality benchmarks
  • Products and services that can stand scrutiny, not just marketing lines
  • Partners who understand that brand-building is a long-term game

Leadership

Founded in 2012, World of Willpower has been shaped by the experience and thinking style of Dr. Jayant Vishnu Bhat, MD (Naturopath), M.Sc Cytogenetics – a founder who has seen multiple sides of business: corporate roles, manufacturing, brand-building, consulting and now a group of ventures under one calm, serious identity.

Jayant has had the opportunity to work with large conglomerates like Troikaa Pharma, Raptakos Brett, ICICI PruLife, MetLife India Reinsurance Group of Americas, Reliance and ING Life at decisive levels. This mindset flows into every Willpower venture – whether it is dairy purity, Ayurvedic formulations, technology systems or financial planning.
