WHAT TYPICALLY BRINGS PEOPLE HERE
  • Founders who want Ayurvedic or herbal brands with real formulations – not copy-paste mixes – and are ready for disciplined work.
  • Professionals who value product documentation, rationale and stability testing instead of “secret recipes” with no paperwork.
