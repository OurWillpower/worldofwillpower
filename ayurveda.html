WHAT TYPICALLY BRINGS PEOPLE HERE
  • Founders who want Ayurvedic or herbal brands with real formulations – not copy-paste mixes – and are ready for disciplined work.
  • Professionals who value product documentation, rationale and stability testing instead of “secret recipes” with no paperwork.
  • Brands keen to expand into wellness, nutraceutical, personal care or skincare lines that respect both tradition and modern quality benchmarks.
  • Partners who want a manufacturing & brand ally that understands ingredient integrity, sourcing discipline and long-term consumer trust.
Work revolves around formulations that can be explained, defended and improved – combining classical wisdom with current expectations on safety and performance.
