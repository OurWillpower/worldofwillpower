Engagement Philosophy

We are selective – and that works better for both sides.

We typically resonate with founders who are willing to invest in quality, documentation and a clear market story – whether the brand is meant for India or global markets.

  • Clarity on which indications you really want to own – not “everything for everyone”.
  • Respect for classical Ayurveda with room for modern formats and compliance.
  • Openness to phased launches – start with a sharp hero product before a full range.
What We Can Co-Create

Depending on the stage of your journey, our role can be limited and sharply defined – or more comprehensive from strategy to execution.

  • Blueprint for your Ayurvedic / herbal product architecture.
  • Transition of an existing formulation into a more potent, differentiated version.
  • Go-to-market thinking – channels, pack sizes, pricing logic and positioning.
