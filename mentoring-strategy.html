Interested in Being Part of an Aggregator Story?

WillPro Aggregators works with manufacturers, brand owners and logistics allies who resonate with this thinking: one disciplined story, many products, many partners – instead of everyone doing their own random experiment.

FOR PARTNERS WHO THINK BEYOND JUST “LISTING ONLINE”.
START AGGREGATOR CONVERSATION ↗

Share who you are – manufacturer, brand, logistics or capital – and we can see if there is a fit in current or future aggregator stories.

WHEN AGGREGATOR STORIES MAKE SENSE
  • You have a good product or capacity but do not want to run a full consumer brand on your own.
  • You are already selling online but know that your catalogue, pricing or communication is scattered.
  • You prefer structured roles – who makes, who owns the brand, who manages logistics and who drives demand.
  • You are willing to operate with clear documentation, predictable quality and transparent numbers.
Aggregator stories need patience and clarity, not shortcuts. We would rather build one strong story than ten weak ones.

How We Typically Structure Aggregators

Every story is different, but the building blocks stay similar – category clarity, role clarity and numbers clarity.

1 · CATEGORY & CUSTOMER
Defining who the aggregator truly serves – which customer, which categories and which price bands – instead of listing “everything”.
2 · PARTNER ROLES
Clear roles for manufacturers, brand custodians, logistics partners and capital providers so efforts are not duplicated or diluted.
3 · SYSTEMS & OPERATIONS
Simple, documentable processes – orders, fulfilment, returns, reviews, catalogue – so the story can actually scale.
